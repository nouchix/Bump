# Bump mobile app — UI kit
Not in the repo yet; designed from the brand brief and the web app's patterns. Two iPhone frames:
- **NowPlayingScreen.jsx** — listener player: cover, title + verified, scrubber, transport, "Buy · 100% to artist", up next.
- **DashboardScreen.jsx** — artist Studio: paid-to-you total, range tabs, plays/sales, recent sales, your tracks. Mini player opens Now playing.
- **MobileChrome.jsx** — MiniPlayer, TabBar (Home / Search / Library / Studio).
Interaction: Buy on the left phone → a sale lands on the right phone with "Someone just paid you directly."
Numbers and names are sample data.
