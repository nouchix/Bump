The Bump logo — use the horizontal lockup in headers, stacked for square formats, mark alone at favicon/avatar sizes.
```jsx
<Logo height={28} />
<Logo variant="mark" tone="signal" height={48} />
<Logo variant="horizontal" tone="light" height={40} />
```
- Clear space: the AirPod head's diameter on every side. Minimum: mark 16px, horizontal 20px tall.
- AirPod is always Signal in two-colour tones. Don't recolour, rotate, outline or put on busy art without a Void scrim.
- Static files live in `assets/logo/`.
